==================================================

DO NOT CHANGE THE EXISTING STRATEGY.

The strategy is:

BUY:

CLOSED 1H bullish engulfing

+
CLOSED 1D bullish

SELL:

CLOSED 1H bearish engulfing
+
CLOSED 4H bearish
+
CLOSED 1D bearish


BULLISH ENGULFING:

Previous 1H candle:

previous.close < previous.open

Current 1H candle:

current.close > current.open

Current candle BODY completely engulfs previous candle BODY:

current.open <= previous.close
AND
current.close >= previous.open


BEARISH ENGULFING:

Previous 1H candle:

previous.close > previous.open

Current 1H candle:

current.close < current.open

Current candle BODY completely engulfs previous candle BODY:

current.open >= previous.close
AND
current.close <= previous.open


WICKS ARE IGNORED.

Only OPEN and CLOSE are used for engulfing.

Do NOT use EMA.

Do NOT use RSI.

Do NOT use MACD.

Do NOT use ADX.

Do NOT use ATR.

Do NOT add any additional indicators or conditions.
